# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Bless a tree with the hammer's repair tool so nothing can fell it; click again to lift the
  blessing. Hovering a tree with the hammer out shows what a click will do.
- A blessed tree shrugs off every hit like the ancient root: no damage, no shaking, no wood
  chips. Looking at it says `Blessed by Odin`.
- Blessing a tree calls down lightning on it, followed by the red burst of a forsaken power
  and a few seconds of the red flicker of an activated boss stone.
- The ground around a blessed tree cannot be dug, raised, levelled or tilled. With the mod on
  the server this holds for every player, with or without the mod.
- Right clicking an unblessed tree with the hammer turns it into the next kind of its family.
- Tapping crouch three times in a row beside a sapling makes it glow green and grow; keep
  twerking and it is a tree in about fifteen seconds.
