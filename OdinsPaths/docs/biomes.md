# Biomes: what to expect and test

What each biome does to a path, and which of it is still an expectation rather than something
seen in game. Facts marked **verify** still need checking in game.

| Biome | Expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Meadows, Black Forest | The easy case. Forests are dense: vegetation clearing matters here most. Seen 2026-09-24: under the trees the dirt is sometimes hard to make out - a per-biome width factor (the Black Forest wider) is the likely answer. |
| Swamp | Low and wet. Much of it is at or just under water level, so the path fords and breaks. Bonemass sits in the swamp's heart. Expect gaps. Seen 2026-09-24: because it is flat, the swamp was the *cheapest* ground at ×1.5 and paths went out of their way through it; now ×3 (`SwampCost`), to tune with `paths costs swamp <n>`. **Causeways** (2026-09-25; the preview marked the road to Bonemass as water end to end): in the Swamp, ground down to 1.5 m under the water (`Trail.SwampFill`) is built up to 0.4 m above it (`CausewayHeight`), painted, and neither the trail nor the preview counts it as water; the search counts it as ground at the swamp's price instead of a ford (×3 again) or a swim. Deeper pools stay water. **Verify:** how the embankment looks (a 1.5 m shoulder for up to 1.9 m of fill), whether the swamp's own water plane shows over it, and that trees on it go. |
| Mountain | **Needs testing.** Moder's altar is on a peak. The slope cost should make switchbacks. The questions are whether the max grade finds any way up at all (fall back to a steeper grade on the last 200 m), whether levelling cuts look bad on steep flanks, and whether dirt shows through the snow texture. |
| Plains | Easy, flat, open. Fuling villages are locations, so the path steers clear. |
| Mistlands | **A dirt track, lit** (second lay, 2026-09-25): no levelling and no stone there, a road post with its mist-clearing lamp every 48 m (`docs/laying.md`; 24 m until the third lay); the search keeps clear of the viaducts' and giant skeletons' real footprint. **In since 2026-09-25**: the Queen's dungeon entrance (`Mistlands_DvergrBossEntrance1`, key `defeated_queen`) is in the default progression, and the Mistlands cost ×8 per metre (×4 until 2026-09-25: the height profile is too broken for a road anywhere it need not go), so a road enters them only for her and the infected mines. **Entries** (2026-09-25, `src/Entries.cs`): a search that had to cross the Mistlands from afar flooded its ellipse, since at 4 m their cliffs cost far more than the 128 m survey sees (the third infected mine: 2.1 million cells, 460 s). Now, around each goal in them, the nearest ground outside them in each of eight directions within 1.5 km - or a Mistlands beach at most 2.5 m above the water with open water 24 m beyond - is an entry; the road is searched to the cheapest entry, and from there to the goal by a search of its own in the small ellipse between them (16 m, then 4 m, at most 400 000 cells). If that finds no way, the road ends at the entry. **Verify** the entries in a preview (the log names each road's entry) and whether a road in reaches the goal. **Verify** the rock formations below. Before: **likely not in the first version.** The base heights are terraced (`GetMistlandsHeight` quantises to 1/400 of the height multiplier of 200, that is 0.5 m steps). The slope cost can handle that. The real walls are the giant rock formations, which are vegetation: invisible to the search in generated zones unless it reads their ZDOs as obstacles, and kept off the path by the clear areas in ungenerated ones. That second part might just work. Default: high cost, the path to the Queen's entrance is config opt in, and the mod page says it is experimental. |
| Ashlands | **In since 2026-09-25**: Fader's altar (`FaderLocation`, `defeated_fader`) is in the default progression, and two charred fortresses (`CharredFortress`) before him. The ground costs ×2, lava (vegetation mask alpha > 0.6 from `GetBiomeHeight(AshLands)`, as `ZoneSystem.IsLavaPreHeightmap` tests it) is impassable in a fine cell and in a wide cell half on it, and dearer by its share below that. Paint keeps alpha, so a road never covers lava. **Verify:** a road there at all (the sea around the Ashlands is a crossing), how stone looks on ash, lava beside the road. |
| Deep North | **Has a boss** in this build: the Frozen King in `DN_Bossroom` (its vegvisir `Vegvisir_DNBoss` pins that location; key `defeated_frozenking`, and `defeated_frozenking_p3` for his last phase), in the default progression since 2026-09-25, and after his altar dirt side roads to the Winding Tunnels (`TheHole01`), two villages (`NorthVillage`, beside the tunnels where there are any) and Mörkhalla (`MorkBorg`), their spurs to the huts, frozen ships, memorial and hot springs (`network.md`). Ground ×1.2. Paths dig out the snow, see `foundations.md`. **Verify** that `DN_Bossroom` is where players fight him and how a road reaches it. |
| Ocean | The landing cost decides. Test a world where Eikthyr's altar is on another island. The line across open water is not straight (the wander noise bends it), which does not matter for a boat, but a landing has to tell the player where the far shore is - see the port markers under "Later" in `../ROADMAP.md`. |
