## 0.1.1

## 0.1.0

- Lightning on the grave: the obliterator's bolt comes down on the fresh tombstone, cosmetic and
  harmless.
- The loser jingle plays out of the grave once the thunder has rolled, with two fading echoes.
- Dying brings a short thunderstorm down on everyone.
- Looting the grave plays the jingle one more time, with a gust of wind.
- All of it is broadcast to every modded client.
