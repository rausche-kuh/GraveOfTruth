# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The compass: craftable at a workbench from a deer trophy and ten stones, worn in the utility
  slot, upgradable through seven more tiers, one per biome, each from that biome's materials
  plus the compass below it. The tooltip lists what a tier knows.
- Worn, the compass seeks its target: a few wavy lines of blue light leave you toward it, low
  over the ground and up over a hill in the way, a little more the closer, running into it as
  you arrive, and none within ten metres (the arrival distance is in the config).
  The status icon names the target, says "not in this world" when the world
  has none, and "none nearby" for ore the compass has not seen yet.
- N switches the compass to its next target; the choice is saved with the character.
