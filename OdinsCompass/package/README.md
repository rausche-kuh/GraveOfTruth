# Odin's Compass

Valheim hides its bosses well. Odin's Compass is a compass you craft, wear like the wishbone,
and follow: a wind of bright blue light blows toward the nearest boss, a little stronger the
closer you get. No map pins, no sound, no spoilers — a direction, and the walk is still yours.

## What it does

- **Craft it** at a workbench from a deer trophy and ten stones. It goes in the utility slot,
  where the wishbone or the wisplight would go.
- **Follow it** — with the compass worn, a few wavy lines of blue light leave you toward what
  it seeks, low over the ground and up over a hill in the way, a few more as you close in,
  running into it as you arrive, and none once you are there.
- **Upgrade it** biome by biome, each tier from the materials of the biome before: Meadows,
  Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, Plains, Mistlands, Ashlands, Deep North. Every tier unlocks that
  biome's boss and its places — burial chambers, sunken crypts, frost caves, tar pits, infested
  mines, charred fortresses — and the tier's ore.
- **Choose** what it seeks with a key (N by default); the compass remembers per character.
- **Ore is what you have seen.** Bosses and dungeons are found anywhere in the world; copper,
  tin and silver only among the ground your game has loaded this session - a vein you walked
  past an hour ago is still found, one beyond the horizon is not.

What each tier seeks and what it costs is in the config, so a server can tune both.

## Multiplayer

Client side; no server install. The compass asks the server the way the vegvisir stones do,
which every server answers, modded or not.

## Install

A mod manager (Gale or r2modman), or drop the zip's contents into
`BepInEx/plugins/rauschekuh-OdinsCompass/`. Requires the BepInEx pack for Valheim.
